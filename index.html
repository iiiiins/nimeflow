Only things with something to do. Bump an episode, say you're caught up, pin what you're watching next, or wave a sequel off for good — every button clears the card, which is what keeps this a queue and not a second library. Or paste a list

Type it the way you'd say it, one per line. "blue lock both seasons completed", "one piece - up to ep 1050 - dropped it", "the greatest estate developer (webtoon)". It parses status, episode counts and medium, then asks you to confirm anything it had to guess. Nothing is saved until you press commit.

Rank — which did you like more?

Press 1 or 2 to pick, 3 for a tie, backspace to undo. Nothing to type. It asks the pairs whose answers it can't already predict, so each one teaches it something — comparing your favourite against something you dropped would teach nothing. Scores are replayed from your answers, never stored, so an undo genuinely corrects the ranking.

Standing

What it can't explain

The recommender scores every title on a handful of dials — does the story punish its lead, does the lead drive the outcome, is there a visible progression system. This downloads which of them predict you, and the titles they get most wrong. Those are the ones no dial covers yet, and they are what a new dial gets written from. It contains title names and numbers, nothing else about your library.

Library

Caught up means you've seen everything released, not that the work is finished — so new seasons resurface in Now. Change status or progress inline; it saves immediately. Movies, specials and OVAs get a verdict instead of a score, because they can't fairly be compared against a full run — that verdict is yours to keep and is weighted zero in the taste model, so a franchise isn't counted twice.

Account and your data

Account

This copy has no server behind it, so there is no account to sign in to and nothing leaves this browser. Everything below still works, and the file it exports opens in any other copy.

Signing in makes your phone and your laptop one library instead of two. Accounts are invite-only: ask whoever sent you this link to invite your email address, and you will get a message asking you to set a password.

You were invited. Choose a password and you are in.

Export

Writes the whole library to a file you keep: titles, progress, scores and every ranking answer. It is the copy that survives a cleared browser, a paused server and a lost account, and it opens in any other copy of NimeFlow, hosted or desktop.

Import

Replaces this library with an exported file. Nothing is merged — two libraries have no rule for whose progress wins — so this says exactly what goes and what arrives before it touches anything. What is here now is snapshotted first and lands in Backups below.

Backups

Snapshots this app takes for itself: before it overwrites the library, before an import, and whenever a sync finds two copies that both changed. Restoring one is itself undoable — what you have now becomes the newest snapshot in the list, so you can go straight back.
