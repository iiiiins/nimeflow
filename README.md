# NimeFlow

A tracker for anime and manga that answers **"what should I watch now?"** instead of just
recording what you already watched.

Open the link. There is nothing to install.

---

## Where your list is kept, in plain words

**Your list is saved to a server I run**, so it follows you from your laptop to your phone
instead of being two separate lists.

That means:

- **I can technically read it.** It is stored as ordinary data on a database I control. I do not
  share it, I do not sell it, and there is no advertising or analytics of any kind in this site.
- **You can take it with you.** The **Data** tab has *Export a copy*, which downloads the whole
  thing as a file. That file is yours and it imports back into any copy of this app.
- **You can have it deleted.** Ask me and it goes.

That is the trade for having one list across your devices. If you would rather nothing left your
machine at all, ask me for the folder version instead: it runs on your own computer, keeps
everything in a file next to itself, and needs no account.

**Until you sign in**, your list lives only in the browser you are using. That is real storage,
but a browser can forget it: clearing site data does it in one click, and a private window never
keeps anything. Export a copy if you are not signed in.

## Getting in

Accounts are **invite-only**, so there is no sign-up button that works. Ask me to invite your
email address and you will get a message asking you to set a password.

If sign-in says the server cannot be reached, that is usually not you. The free hosting this runs
on goes to sleep after a week with nobody using it. Tell me and I will wake it. Your list is
safe on your device either way, and everything except syncing keeps working.

## What makes it different

Every tracker is a ledger. It stores what you watched and hands you the same recommendations
everyone else gets, which are mostly whatever is popular.

**It knows what you are owed.** Not just "finished" and "watching", but *caught up*: you have
seen everything released and a new season would be news. When one drops, it tells you.

**It learns your taste from comparisons, not stars.** People are bad at "rate this out of ten"
and good at "which of these two did you prefer". It asks pairs. After a while it can predict
which of two shows you would pick, and it recommends on that.

**It does not rank by community score.** It measures whether community score predicts *your*
ratings, and it shows you that number. For some people it is positive. For others it is
negative, meaning the highest-rated shows are reliably the ones they dropped. Either way the
recommendations follow you and not the crowd.

## Your first twenty minutes

**Paste your history** into **Add**, one per line, the way you would say it out loud:

```
attack on titan all seasons
one piece - up to ep 1050 - dropped it, got bored
solo leveling both seasons and the manga
frieren
chainsaw man, watching
```

It works out the title, whether you finished it, and how far you got, then shows you its guesses
and asks you to confirm. Nothing is saved until you press commit.

Thirty titles is a good start. This is the part everything else depends on.

**Rank some pairs** in **Rank**. It shows two things you have seen and asks which you liked more.
Press `1` or `2`, `3` for a tie, `backspace` to undo. Recommendations switch on after three
titles are ranked, and the ranking stops moving around 150 answers. You do not have to do it in
one sitting.

**Then look at Discover.** *For you* is one list ranked against your whole profile. *More like…*
gives you a shelf per favourite.

## Honest limits

**It needs your history before it is any good.** With nothing in it, it can do nothing.

**Chapter counts for ongoing manga are often missing.** The databases only publish a chapter
count once a series finishes. Where a number can be found it is shown; where it cannot, the app
says so rather than guessing.

**Sequel counts are a floor.** "24+ episodes across 2+ seasons" means the data reaches one step
along the chain and there may be more.

**It gets things wrong.** The Rank tab has a **Download fit report** button showing which parts
of the model predict you and which titles it gets most wrong. Send me that file and it can be
improved for you specifically.
